# 2048 Cupcakes

A fan-made bakery edition of the 2048 sliding-tile puzzle. Thirteen bakes, from a vanilla birthday cupcake at 2 to a rainbow swirl at 8192.

Una edición de repostería: trece dulces, del cupcake de cumpleaños de vainilla en el 2 al remolino arcoíris en el 8192.

**Play:** [English](https://tile2048.github.io/2048-CUPCAKES/) · [Español](https://tile2048.github.io/2048-CUPCAKES/es/)

## How to play

Arrow keys move the whole board; a swipe does the same on a phone. Two tiles of the same value merge into the next one on the ladder, and the value of the new tile is added to your score. The run ends when the board is full and no two neighbours match, so leave a corner free and plan one merge ahead.
