How to play: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two tiles with the same symbol touch, they get promoted!

The counter holds thirteen bakes and the tray is a 4×4 grid. A vanilla birthday cupcake opens at 2; the menu then climbs through bubblegum pink, sunshine vanilla, Valrhona blonde ganache, chocolate peanut butter cheesecake, chocolate mint fudge, chocolate spider web, toasted marshmallow, cookies and creme and chocolate sundae, on to white chocolate peppermint at 2048, 2017 Confetti Vanilla, and the rainbow swirl at 8192. Slide with the arrow keys or a swipe; two identical cupcakes become the next one and their calories are added to your score. Win at the 2048 peppermint, then keep going for the rainbow. A full tray with no matching pair closes the shop.
